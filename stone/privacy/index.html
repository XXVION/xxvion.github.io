1. 개요

돌멩이 차기(이하 “앱”)는 걸은 만큼 돌을 차서 다듬고, 다듬은 돌로 마을을 짓는 게임입니다. 개발자 XXVION(이하 “개발자”)은 이용자의 개인정보를 소중히 여기며, 이 방침에서 앱이 어떤 정보를 어떻게 다루는지 설명합니다.

앱은 회원가입과 로그인이 없고, 인터넷 권한이 없습니다. 앱이 읽는 걸음 수와 게임 기록은 기기 안에만 저장되며, 개발자의 서버나 다른 회사로 전송되지 않습니다. 앱에는 광고, 인앱 결제, 분석·추적 도구가 없습니다.

3. Health Connect 정보의 사용 제한

Health Connect에서 읽은 걸음 수는 이 방침에 적은 용도(게임 진행과 걸음 통계 표시)로만 씁니다. 광고나 그 밖의 목적으로 쓰지 않으며, 다른 앱·서비스·제3자에게 옮기거나 판매하지 않습니다.

1. Overview

돌멩이 차기 (“the app”) is a game in which your steps kick and polish a stone, and polished stones build a village. This policy explains how the app, developed by XXVION (“we”), handles information.

The app has no accounts or sign-in and no internet permission. The step counts it reads and your game records are stored only on your device and are never sent to our servers or to any other company. The app contains no ads, no in-app purchases and no analytics or tracking tools.

2. Information processed only on your device

InformationHow it is readWhat it is used for
Step countStep records in Health Connect (read-only) and the device’s step sensor (Physical activity permission)To kick and polish your stone as you walk. Steps are also read in the background so that steps taken while the app is closed are counted.
Game recordsCreated by the appThe stone in progress and your collected stones, your village, shop decorations, daily step statistics and your friend code, stored on the device to show them in the app.
SettingsCreated by the appSound, vibration, notification and similar preferences, stored on the device.

The app does not read any health data other than steps (such as heart rate, weight or sleep) and does not write to or delete from Health Connect. It does not read your location.

This data is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. If Android backup is turned on, it may be included in your Google account backup like other app data. That backup is handled by Google according to your settings, and we cannot see its contents.

3. Limited use of Health Connect data

Step counts read from Health Connect are used only for the purposes described in this policy (game progress and step statistics). They are not used for advertising or any other purpose, and are not transferred or sold to other apps, services or third parties.

4. Sharing and friend codes

The app does not share or sell any information to third parties.

When you tap “Send invite code” on the Friends screen, the app hands a short text containing your friend code (in the form STONE-XXXX, generated on the device) to the app you choose, such as a messenger. The text leaves your device only when you send it yourself and contains no name, contact details or step counts.
