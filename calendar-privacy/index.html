개인정보처리방침

포지 캘린더(forj. Calendar) (이하 "앱")는 이용자의 개인정보를 소중히 다룹니다. 이 방침은 앱이 어떤 정보를 다루고, 어디에 보관하며, 어떻게 지울 수 있는지 설명합니다.

  • 시행일: 2026-11-11
  • 개발자: XXVION Studio
  • 문의: xxvion.studio@gmail.com

1. 핵심 요약

  • 앱 개발자는 별도의 서버를 운영하지 않습니다. 이용자의 일정 데이터는 개발자에게 전송되지 않습니다.
  • 일정·할 일 등 이용자가 입력한 내용은 이용자의 기기에 저장되고, 동기화나 백업을 켜면 이용자 본인의 Google Drive "앱 전용 폴더" 에만 저장됩니다.
  • 앱은 이용자 Google Drive의 다른 파일에는 접근할 수 없습니다(앱 전용 폴더 권한만 요청).

2. 처리하는 정보

  • 프로필 사진은 받지 않습니다.
  • 로그인 과정에서 Google이 발급하는 인증 토큰은 기기 안에서만 쓰며, 저장하거나 개발자에게 보내지 않습니다.
  • 위 정보는 개발자에게 전송되지 않습니다.

2-3. 앱 설정

시작 요일, 테마, 언어, 알림 설정, 광고 미션 진행 상황 등은 이용자 기기에만 저장됩니다.

2-4. 광고

앱은 무료 이용을 위해 Google AdMob 광고(하단 배너, 이용자가 직접 누르는 보상형 광고)를 표시합니다. AdMob은 광고 제공·측정·부정 방지를 위해 기기의 광고 ID, 광고 노출·클릭 정보, 기기·앱 진단 정보, IP 주소 등을 수집해 Google에 보낼 수 있습니다. 앱 개발자는 이 정보를 직접 받거나 저장하지 않습니다. 자세한 내용은 Google의 개인정보처리방침(https://policies.google.com/privacy)과 광고 기술 안내(https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)를 참고하세요. 이용자는 기기 설정에서 광고 ID를 재설정하거나 삭제할 수 있습니다. "광고 제거"를 구매한 이용자는 다음 실행부터 광고 SDK를 시작하지 않으므로 AdMob이 위 정보를 수집하지 않습니다. 이 앱은 현재 대한민국에서만 배포됩니다. 유럽(EEA·영국·스위스)으로 배포를 넓힐 때는 그 지역 이용자에게 맞춤 광고 동의 화면을 먼저 보여 주도록 이 방침을 개정합니다.

2-5. 결제

유료 상품("광고 제거")은 Google Play 결제로 처리됩니다. 결제 정보(카드 번호 등)는 Google이 처리하며 앱 개발자는 받지 않습니다. 앱은 구매 여부(광고 제거 권한)만 기기에 저장하고, 앱을 시작하거나 다시 열 때 Google Play에서 구매 내역을 확인해 재설치·환불을 반영합니다. 앱은 구매 정보를 별도 서버로 보내지 않습니다.

Privacy Policy (English)

forj. Calendar ("the App") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the App handles, where it is stored, and how you can delete it.

  • Effective date: 2026-11-11
  • Developer: XXVION Studio
  • Contact: xxvion.studio@gmail.com

1. Summary

  • The developer does not operate any server. Your calendar data is never sent to the developer.
  • What you enter (events, to-dos, etc.) is stored on your device, and, if you turn on sync or backup, only in the app-specific folder of your own Google Drive.
  • The App cannot access any other files in your Google Drive (it requests the app-data folder scope only).

2. Information we handle

2-1. Google account information (sign-in)

Through Google Sign-In (Android Credential Manager), the App receives:

ItemPurposeStored
Email addressIdentify the signed-in account, show it in SettingsOn your device
Name (display name)Show the account in SettingsOn your device
  • The App does not receive your profile photo.
  • The authentication token issued by Google is used only on the device; it is not stored or sent to the developer.
  • This information is not sent to the developer.

2-2. Content you enter

Events (title, date and time, repeat rule, memo, reminders, subtasks, completion), to-dos, groups (name, color), people you register (name only), and places you type (name).

  • Stored in the App's private storage on your device.
  • If you turn on sync or backup, it is saved as files (one sync file, one backup file) in the app-data folder of your own Google Drive. These files also contain a random device identifier generated by the App to tell devices apart.
  • The developer cannot access this content.

2-3. App settings

Start of week, theme, language, notification settings and ad-mission progress are stored only on your device.

2-4. Ads

The App shows Google AdMob ads (a bottom banner and rewarded ads you choose to watch). To serve, measure and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, ad impressions and clicks, device and diagnostic information and IP address, and send them to Google. The developer does not receive or store this information. See Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services (https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads). You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. If you buy "Remove ads", the App stops starting the ads SDK from the next launch, so AdMob no longer collects this information.

2-5. Payments

Purchases ("Remove ads") are processed by Google Play. Payment details are handled by Google and are not received by the developer. The App stores only whether you own the purchase (ad removal) on your device, and checks your purchases with Google Play when it starts or comes back to the foreground to reflect reinstalls and refunds. The App does not send purchase data to any server of its own.

4. Third-party services

Google Sign-In (Credential Manager / Identity Services), Google Drive API (only when you turn on sync or backup), Google AdMob (ads; collects the advertising ID and related data), Google Play Billing (purchases and purchase checks). All provided by Google LLC. The App uses no other analytics or crash-reporting tools. The App is currently distributed in the Republic of Korea only; if distribution expands to the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, users there will be shown a personalized-ads consent screen first and this policy will be updated. All network traffic is encrypted with HTTPS.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Account info on the device (email, name): removed when you sign out.
  • Calendar data on the device: stays after sign-out; removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data (Settings › Apps › forj. Calendar › Storage › Clear data).
  • Sync and backup files in Google Drive: delete them from your Google account — Google Drive (web) › Settings › Manage apps › forj. Calendar › Delete hidden app data.
  • Deletion records: to tell your other devices about a deletion, only the item's identifier and time are kept for up to 90 days, then removed automatically.
  • The App does not use Android Auto Backup or device-to-device transfer; a reinstall starts empty until you choose "Restore from cloud".
  • The developer stores no data on any server, so there is nothing to request deletion of from the developer. Questions: xxvion.studio@gmail.com

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 14 (under 13 for users outside Korea) and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will announce it in the App or on this page and update the effective date.
